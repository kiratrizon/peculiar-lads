🫶 THE PECU CODE OF ETHICS 🫶



Good day, PECU! ❤️



As one of your officers, I wanted to bring something meaningful from my profession into our little gaming community.



Peculiar Lads isn't just about gear, damage, rankings, raids, or who grinds the hardest. 😂 We've created friendships here. We raid together, celebrate achievements together, help each other, argue sometimes, make up, and create memories together.



🌟 THE PECU CODE OF ETHICS 🌟



This isn't meant to make our guild overly serious or strict.

It's simply a reminder of the kind of community I hope we continue building together.



❤️ 1. BENEFICENCE — DO GOOD



Beneficence reminds us to contribute to the well-being of others.



The PECU Way: Help your guildmates when you can.



Share knowledge. Help someone who's struggling. Welcome new members. Celebrate someone's achievement instead of competing with everything they accomplish.

Sometimes something small to you can mean a lot to another member.

If you have the opportunity to make someone's gaming experience better, choose kindness.



🕊️ 2. NONMALEFICENCE — DO NO HARM



Our words and actions can affect other people—even inside a game.



Disagreements are normal.



Raid teams require cooperation. Sometimes we're going to disagree with our teammates, raid leaders, strategies, or decisions.

Talk about the problem. Ask an officer to mediate if necessary. Take some time away if emotions are high.



Personal conflict should never become collective punishment.



When we enter that raid together, remember:



⚔️ WE ARE A TEAM.



Whatever differences we may have, no harm should intentionally come to another guild member because of them.



🔐 3. AUTONOMY — RESPECT CHOICES, BOUNDARIES & PRIVACY



Every PECU has the right to make their own decisions.



You can ask another member for information.

You can ask about their game account.

You can offer suggestions.

You can ask someone to join your team.



But when someone says NO, declines, or shows that they're uncomfortable sharing something...



Respect their decision and step back.



Nobody should feel pressured to provide account information, personal information, passwords, or anything else they aren't comfortable sharing.



Don't repeatedly ask.
Don't guilt-trip them.
Don't make them feel like refusing means they don't trust the guild.



And if someone DOES trust you with private information, protect that trust.



Don't share another person's information or private conversations without their permission.

Privacy doesn't require an explanation.

Trust is earned. Respect it.



⚖️ 4. JUSTICE — BE FAIR



Every PECU deserves fair and respectful treatment.



Whether someone is an officer, veteran member, new recruit, high-level player, casual player, whale, or F2P —basic respect should remain the same.



During guild events, raids, rewards, competitions, and other activities, decisions should be made as fairly as possible.



Personal friendships should not become an excuse to mistreat someone else.

We don't have to treat everyone identically, but everyone deserves fairness.



💬 5. VERACITY — BE HONEST



Trust cannot exist without honesty.



Be truthful with your guildmates.



Don't deliberately spread false information, create rumors, twist someone's words, or misrepresent a situation to make another member look bad.

When conflict happens, tell the truth—even when the truth doesn't completely favor your side.



And if you make a mistake?



Own it.

Sometimes a simple:



"I was wrong."
"I misunderstood."
"I'm sorry."



can prevent days of unnecessary guild drama.

Honesty builds trust.



🤝 6. FIDELITY — BE LOYAL TO THE TRUST WE BUILD



Being part of a community means having responsibilities toward one another.

Keep your commitments when reasonably possible.

If you commit to a raid team, remember that other people may be depending on you.



⏰ RESPECT EACH OTHER'S TIME.



Please try to be ready and available at the agreed raid time. If you're running late, can't attend, or something unexpected comes up, communicate with your team as early as possible.



Remember that your teammates may have also set aside time from their day specifically for the raid. A simple message or heads-up shows consideration for everyone who is waiting and depending on the team.



Of course, real life always comes first, and unexpected situations happen. No one should be punished or made to feel guilty for having responsibilities outside the game.

We simply ask that, whenever possible, communicate with your team.

Respect their time just as you would want them to respect yours. ❤️

If something happens and you can't participate, let your team know instead of suddenly disappearing or intentionally leaving everyone behind.



Respect confidential conversations.

Respect the team. Respect the commitment. Respect each other's time. 🤝



🌎 PROFESSIONALISM — REMEMBER THAT YOU REPRESENT PECU



This one is especially important when we're interacting with other guilds.

You are always free to joke, have fun, and be yourself.

But please know when to act accordingly.



When dealing with other guilds, raid groups, alliances, or players, try to represent yourself and Peculiar Lads respectfully.



Even if another player is disrespectful...

You don't always have to match their behavior.

Sometimes the strongest response is simply showing that we can be better than that.

Don't create unnecessary conflicts between entire guilds because of an argument between a few individuals.



Be respectful.
Be mature.
Compete hard.
Win gracefully.
Lose gracefully.



And let people recognize Peculiar Lads not only because we're strong players, but because we're good people to play with.



❤️ DIGNITY — REMEMBER THE PERSON BEHIND THE CHARACTER



This is probably the value I want everyone to remember the most.



Behind every character...

there is a person.



A person who may have had a difficult day.
A person with responsibilities outside the game.
A person who may interpret a joke differently from you.
A person who deserves basic dignity and respect.



You don't have to like everybody.
You don't have to agree with everybody.
You don't even have to be friends with everybody.



But you can still treat them like a human being.



🗣️ WHEN CONFLICT HAPPENS: COMMUNICATE BEFORE YOU ESCALATE



PECU, we're not going to pretend disagreements will never happen.

They will. 😂

We're different people with different personalities.

If something bothers you, communicate.

If possible, talk privately with the person involved instead of immediately making the situation public.

Listen to their side.

Explain yours.



If you cannot resolve it, approach an officer and allow us to help mediate fairly.

And once something has genuinely been resolved...



let people move forward.



Don't turn every mistake into a permanent grudge.



🌟 THE PECU WAY 🌟



❤️ Beneficence — Choose kindness and help each other.
🕊️ Nonmaleficence — Don't intentionally harm another member or the team.
🔐 Autonomy — Respect choices, boundaries, and privacy.
⚖️ Justice — Treat people fairly.
💬 Veracity — Be truthful.
🤝 Fidelity — Protect trust, honor your commitments, and respect each other's time.



And above all...



🎮 ONE LAST THING, PECU...



At the end of the day...



THIS IS A GAME.



We're supposed to be here to relax, grind, raid, get stronger, collect things, laugh at each other, complain about RNG 😂, and create memories.



We're not asking anyone to be perfect.



We're simply asking everyone to contribute to making Peculiar Lads a community worth logging into every day.



We may fight sometimes.
We may disagree.
We may annoy each other. 😂



But at the end of the day...



We're PECU.



We're guildmates.
We're teammates.
And in our own weird, peculiar way...

— ADMIN
