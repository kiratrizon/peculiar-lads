# 📢 **PeculiarLads Bot — v2 Update**

More upgrades since the last update — here's what's new!

## ✅ Onboarding Now Gates Every Command

If you're a new member and haven't finished onboarding yet (posted your application in the onboarding channel, or applied through the website), **no bot command will work for you yet** — you'll get a reminder instead.

Once onboarding is done and your applicant role is lifted, everything unlocks automatically.

---

## 🏆 QA Points & Leveling

The QA channel now rewards good answers.

Help others out in there and you'll start earning points — rack up enough and you level up, with a congratulations message and a custom card image posted right in the QA channel.

**Leveling also unlocks roles:**

* **Level 5** → QA Contributor
* **Level 15** → QA Regular
* **Level 30** → QA Expert

Roles stack — reaching Level 30 means you keep all three.

---

## 🔑 New: `/signup` and `/login`

Joined entirely through Discord with `/character add` and never touched the website? You can still get a full account:

* **`/signup`** — get your personal signup link to set a password on the website.
* **`/login`** — once you've signed up, get your login link anytime.

If you're already signed up, `/signup` will just point you to `/login` instead.

---

## 🔍 New: `/scammer scan`

Want to check someone before trading or inviting them? Run `/scammer scan [ign]` to quickly check whether a specific IGN is on the scammer list — no need to scroll through the full `/scammer list` anymore.
